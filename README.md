# OLFU Kiosk Queue Management System

The OLFU Kiosk Queue Management System helps students and visitors join and track queues at university offices. Through a touch-screen kiosk, they can select an office and applicable service or student level, receive a queue ticket, and follow their progress. A public display shows currently served and waiting tickets.

Staff use the system to call and manage tickets at assigned windows. Queue configurations support general, service-based, and student-level queues, including SHS, College, or both. Administrators can manage offices, services, staff access, and queue settings, and review operational reports.
